Camping Season

You find a good looking area devoid of spinach trees and gather some wood. You make a fire and sit and wait for around an hour and a half, as well as eating some spinach snacks you swiped from the store.

Feeling better rested, you put out the fire and make your way up the mountain. In the darkness of the spinach trees you hear sounds around you, but you can’t be sure if it’s just the wind rustling leaves on the spinach trees. Just in case, you grab a sturdy looking branch to carry with you as you walk.

Grabbing the branch was a good decision, as one of the spinach clumps you step over suddenly starts to aggressively shake, and you leap back as a spinach monster bursts out of the ground. The spinach monster has a spinach bunch as a head and has arms and legs made of roots, it seems around 4 and half feet tall. You take the initiative and go for the attack, and you and the monster trade a few blows, but the monster manages to get a good hit and you fly back into a spinach tree, losing your branch. The beast leaps at you, and as you close your eyes and throw out your hands in front of you, preparing for the end, you hear a whoosh followed by silence.

You open your eyes and find the monster several feet away, knocked down, as well as several spinach leaves floating to the ground. The monster gets up and runs away. You continue up the mountain, albeit in confusion about what happened.

Just as the sky begins to brighten slightly, you burst through some spinach bushes and into a clearing at the top of the mountain. There's a cottage in the clearing, a pond with spinach growing out of it, and a cliff to your right. You spot the Old Man in baggy robes made of spinach doing what seems to be Tai Chi. You shout that you've made it, and he congratulates you.

Officially his disciple, you rest in a spare bedroom in his cottage, and in the morning explains that he is a Spinach-tsu master, someone trained in the art of Spinach-tsu. Spinach-tsu is derived from and made to mimic the powers of the Spinach one. You mention what happened on the way up the mountain, and he says it’s likely accidental use of your powers as the Spinach One. He says it’s time to begin training, and asks if you're ready.
