The Story Begins

Yet another day working at the Spinach Central's Central for Spinach, the corner store between asparagus street and brachiosaurus lane. You`ve been working here for almost three months, after graduating college with a degree in paleontology. A degree that you didn`t realize would be as useless as it would be until you had to get a job at a corner store.

Speaking of paleontology, you seem to have dug up a fossil as an old man with a long, white beard enters the store, eyeing you suspiciously. He approaches you, leaning in close, and with a raspy voice says: “Hey sonny boy, what's cool with it?” Bewildered but at this point used to random crazy people, you ask “Is there anything I can get you?”. Ignoring you, he proclaims “I am the Spinach-tsu master that lives on top of Mount Spinach, and I am here so you can begin your Spinach-tsu journey!” “Sir this is a Central for Spinach, do you want some spinach or something? We just got a shipment of spinach flavored carrots,” you reply. The Old Man stares at you for a second, before handing over 5 spinach dollars and taking a bag of spinach carrots. Eating some spinach carrots, he says “You are the spinachosen one! It is time for you to come with me to train your Spinach-tsu and fulfill your destiny!” You look at the time, you're about to clock out anyway, and you wonder if maybe you should just go with him. Life has been boring at the corner store. On the other hand, this guy is clearly crazy, and there's no way this ends well.
