Over the hills and through the woods

There’s no way you're going across the lake, you've heard way too many rumors about monsters filling Lake Spinach and you're not taking any chances. The right path around the lake looks like it could be shorter than the left path, so you decide to go that way. After some time, you realize that the path descends into swamp land, but press on anyway. The journey through the swamp takes a long time, and before long it’s dark out. You take a rest and eat some spinach snacks as you come out the other side of the swamp, and cross the bridge over Spinach River, the one that flows from Lake Spinach eastward to the ocean. The rest of your journey is uneventful, and you pass through the forest following the bridge just fine. It’s been several hours at this point, and it’s hard to tell the time. Through the swamp, over the bridge, and through the forest, you now stand at the foot of Spinach Mountain. Feeling good, you set off.


As you climb the mountain, you unfortunately realise that a lot of the east side of the mountain is cliffs, so you'll have to take a long way around the mountain in order to get up. As you hike up the mountain, you look around in wonder at the Spinach trees that grow around you. You’ve heard about them of course, but they only grow on the mountain range. The rest of your journey is uneventful. After what seems to be a couple more hours you're nearly at the top of the mountain, and are above the sparse clouds dotting the sky. As you make the final stretch, the sky is brightening and you realize you underestimated how much time had passed. The sun is peaking out over the horizon, and you see a break in the spinach trees up ahead. You burst through the end of the forest to the clearing at the top of the mountain, and see a nice looking cottage with a pond, and one side of the clearing ending with a cliff. You spot the Old Man doing what seems to be some sort of Tai Chi in some baggy robe made of spinach. You shout “I’m here Old Man!”. The Old Man turns and eyes you disdainfully, before saying it’s after sunrise. Your expression starts to fall before he promptly throws a hand out at you, and from within the darkness of the baggy sleeve of his robe a torrent of spinach leaves fly out. They consume your vision as you feel yourself being blown off the cliff.

THE END
