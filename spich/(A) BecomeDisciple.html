Lambospineegi

You reluctantly play along, and agree to “become his disciple" so he can get out of your face. At once, he tells you to meet him at the abandoned lakeside docks, and proceeds to throw a clump of spinach at the ground, which explodes in a cloud and he disappears. You are shocked at what just happened, because you thought he was just a random homeless guy. This turn of events leads you to reconsider what he had said before, and you decide what he had said could be true.


30 minutes later you stand at the docks. All of the wood looks like it's rotting, and there's spiderwebs everywhere. From the darkness the Old Man emerges, and he looks at you seriously. “Before I train you, you need to prove yourself. Meet me at the top of Mount Spinach before sunrise the next morning, and I will show you how to reach your full spinach potential. It is six pm now, I recommend you make haste.” This time he just walks off around the corner. You follow him around the corner after a second to see where he goes, and he gets in a lambospineegi sitting outside the docks, revs the engine a few times, and drives off. Returning your attention to the task he gave you, you think about what you know of local geography. The docks on the west side of the city border the Spinach lake, across said lake lies Mount Spinach in the Spynaches mountain range. You could go across the lake, and you even spot some rickety looking canoes you could try to use. You also see two paths that go around the lake, to your left and right. The right path is likely faster as that side of the lake is smaller, but it looks like it descends into a swamp. To your left is a paved road that looks much nicer, but to your knowledge it would take much longer to get around the lake.
