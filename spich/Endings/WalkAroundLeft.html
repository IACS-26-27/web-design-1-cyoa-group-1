Goin' Left Cause This Ain't Right

There’s no way you're going across the lake, you've heard way too many rumors about monsters filling Lake Spinach and you're not taking any chances. You Decide to walk down the left path, as it seems much easier to traverse despite likely being a longer distance than the right path, maybe you will make up the time you would lose from trudging through the swamp. As the sun begins to set, you realize that this path unfortunately leads to the opposite side of the Spynaches from Mount Spinach. You had forgotten to consider that. You decide to keep going forwards, as going back would definitely take too long, this is your only chance to continue forward. The path ends at the base of Mount Cave, which happens to have a giant cave entrance. You peer into the cave and catch a glimpse of faraway sunlight. Perhaps the cave goes fully through the mountain. That would be the shortest path to get past this mountain, and time is of the essence. You decide to step into the dark cave, and use the distant light to guide you. You keep tripping over rocks sticking out of the ground, because you can’t see anything around you. The end of the tunnel seems to be nearing, but suddenly vanishes from your sight. Confused, you try to walk in the direction you remember the light being, but after a few steps you walk straight into something. It couldn’t have been the wall of the cave, it was too soft. You reach out to feel it with your hands, when you suddenly feel it moving, as a pair of glowing eyes spin into your vision from high above you. You seem to have encountered the undiscovered dweller of this cave. You take a step back, but fail to see its arm reaching out to grab you in the dark.

THE END.
