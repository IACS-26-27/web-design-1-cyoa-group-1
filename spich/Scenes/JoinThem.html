Turn to da dark side

You decide to join the Kale Conquerors, partly because you have no love for spinach, and because there's no way you're going to lose this job. It’s been 3 months, and over that time you have trained in the use of Kale weaponry, and have been taught how to counter the enemy. Speaking of, the invasion of Spinachopolis isn't uncontested.

You remember from several weeks ago what you were told, “The City of Spinachopolis is protected by the Spinach Masters, a secretive group of experts in the mysterious techniques known as spinach-tsu.” You never actually saw any of them until the day of the invasion, and the stories you were told were in no way exaggerated. The fighting between the Kale Conquerors and the Spinach Masters was fierce, keyword being was. As the sounds of battle slowly die, you realize it’s been over an hour since you have seen anyone, enemy or ally.

As you cautiously walk around the city, suddenly out of an alleyway steps an old man. He tenses at the sight of you, before his eyes flash in recognition. The old man straightens, and looks down his nose at you. “You made a poor choice, this path shall only lead to your end. You Kale Conquerors have committed an atrocity, and in the process have destroyed not only this city but yourselves. You and I are the only ones left in this city, Spinachosen one.”

Taking several steps back, your eyes widen. You were specifically warned about this spinach master, the Old Man of Spinach Mountain. Of all the spinach masters, this is the one you were told to run from if you met him. “In our duel, I shall put an end to this attack, once and for all.” His words shoot through you, and you realize that if you want to make a decision about what to do next, you need to do so fast, before he chooses for you.
