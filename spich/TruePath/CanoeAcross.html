Lake luck

You eye the lake, seeing it covered in fog. Crossing the lake directly via canoe is likely to be your fastest option, the other paths would take too long. You gather an oar and bring a canoe into the lake, setting off at once. As you paddle, the mist slowly surrounds your vision, and you lose your sense of direction. Still, it should be possible to maintain your trajectory towards the other side of the lake if you keep paddling steadily. After another ten minutes, you hear a splash behind you. You spin around, and see some ripples in the water. Another splash comes from in front of you, and by the time you turn your head its only ripples. You peer down into the water and see dark shapes moving around. All of a sudden you remember why almost nobody uses Spinach Lake. The lake is rumored to be overrun with spinach monsters. Regretting your decision, you start paddling faster. There's a shape in the water ahead of you, and as it rises you recognise what it is. It’s a spinach crocodile! As you realize it and fear starts to shoot through you, the boat shakes as something hits the bottom of it aggressively. Paddling full speed now, you look around in terror and see several spinach crocodiles approaching. Two crocodiles approach you from the side, and when they get close enough you bash them on the head with the oar, and they slink away into the water. More and more spinach crocodiles start appearing out of the depths and prowl towards you, more than you could possibly bash. You close your eyes as you keep paddling as hard as you physically can, and wish somebody could help you.


Suddenly the water around you goes quiet, and you open your eyes. All of the spinach crocodiles have frozen in the water, their attention not longer on you. And all of a sudden the silence breaks, and the crocodiles thrash as they swim in every direction. You're confused before a long ribbon of spinach explodes out of the water, and summarily 3 more burst out all around soon after. Screaming, you find new found strength to draw from as you keep paddling. The spinach tentacles grab several crocodiles and drag them down into the depths of the lake. You gain some distance from the commotion and fueled by adrenaline you strive towards the shore, which is now in sight. A short time later you make land, and scramble out of the canoe, collapsing onto the ground. You take some deep breaths and calm down, and take a look at your surroundings. It seems like luckily you maintained great direction, as you're nearly perfectly where you wanted to land. After a short but tiring walk towards the mountain, you stand at the base of Spinach Mountain, right before where it starts to spire out of the ground. You can see what seems to be an overgrown walking path that should take you up the mountain, and you realize that it’s gone completely dark by now. You want to make sure you arrive in time, but you're exhausted from the journey across the lake and aren't sure you'll make it up. Should you go up now to ensure you get there in time, taking no risks, or should you stop and camp for a little while so you can recover your strength? Who knows what dangers lie on the mountain if the lake was like that.
